EduAniAI (EduAnim AI Pro)

"Turning Static Textbooks into Interactive Movies."

🚀 What is EduAniAI? EduAniAI (Developed by Reema) is an advanced AI text-to-video pipeline designed to simplify education for students in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities across India. It converts boring NCERT chapters, textbook page captures, or complex prompts into dynamic, animated, and interactive video lectures in seconds.

⚙️ How it Works: Vision/Input Captures ➔ Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API Scripting ➔ gTTS Audio Sync ➔ Python MoviePy & FFmpeg Ultrafast Rendering Presets (.mp4 output).
